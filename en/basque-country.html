Why visit

Walk the riverfront past the Guggenheim in Bilbao, then cross to the old town for pintxos, small snacks served at the bar. Near Zumaia, tilted beds of rock run into the sea. At Gaztelugatxe a stone bridge leads to a stairway on an islet.

For a mountain day, choose between the steep climb up Txindoki and the pastures of Urbia. Beyond the mountains, vineyards surround the walled town of Laguardia.

When to visit

Spring and autumn suit coastal walks and city visits. Wet limestone and sandstone can be slippery. Check the local tide table before approaching a wave-cut platform.

In summer plan coastal parking ahead. In winter, snow, mist and short daylight can rule out exposed ridges. Bilbao and San Sebastián offer museums for wet days.

Travel essentials

Road signs often use two names: Donostia is San Sebastián and Gasteiz is Vitoria. Walk into the old quarters from a car park and check low-emission zone rules before driving into a city centre. Driving estimates end at the arrival point; allow separate time for the walk.
