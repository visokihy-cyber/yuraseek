Why visit

El Chaltén and Torres del Paine are the main hiking destinations. Views of Fitz Roy, Cerro Torre and the Paine towers require time on the trail and suitable weather. Lake viewpoints and shorter Torres del Paine walks offer mountain scenery without a major climb.

Perito Moreno’s walkways bring you close to the ice, while boats on lakes Argentino and Grey approach glacier walls. The Carretera Austral links rainforest, Queulat’s hanging glacier, the Marble Caves and remote villages. Around Bariloche in the north, lake drives and woodland walks are easier to combine.

For wildlife, head to Valdés Peninsula, Punta Tombo and penguin colonies near the Strait of Magellan. Cueva de las Manos, Punta Arenas and Caleta Tortel add ancient history, maritime heritage and remote community life to the natural scenery.

When to visit

November–March: the main season for southern hikes, boat trips and the Carretera Austral. Days are long, but strong winds, rain and mountain snow remain possible in summer. Book accommodation, ferries and popular excursions early for January and February.

April and October: fewer visitors and seasonal colours, but some trails and services have closed or not yet reopened. April brings autumn woodland; high routes can retain snow in October.

May–September: winter mountain conditions, short southern days and reduced boat and refuge services. Valdés whale season, however, begins around June and runs into December. Magellanic penguins are generally seen from September or October to March or April, with dates varying by colony.

Travel essentials

Argentina uses the Argentine peso and Chile the Chilean peso: they are different currencies. Cards are common in major tourist centres, but cash is useful in small villages and at car parks. Spanish is the main language. English is common among tour operators but less dependable away from tourism hubs.

Both countries drive on the right. Arrange cross-border permission and insurance with your rental company in advance, and check the chosen border crossing’s hours. When entering Chile, declare animal and plant products under SAG’s rules.

Long stretches between settlements may have no mobile coverage, fuel or shops. Download maps, carry water and charge devices before leaving. Bring wind and rain protection, a warm layer and sun protection; pack insect repellent if needed. Socket types are C/I in Argentina and C/L in Chile, with a 220 V supply.

The main natural hazards are sudden weather changes, cold water and strong wind. Pumas inhabit steppe and parks: do not approach or run if you encounter one, and follow ranger advice. Keep valuables out of sight in parked cars. Check park tickets, refuge bookings and ferry arrangements before leaving a town while you still have a connection.
