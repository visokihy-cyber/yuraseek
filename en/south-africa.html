Why visit

Ocean and mountains. Cape Point, Robberg and the Drakensberg offer different landscapes. Short viewpoints, walks and substantial hikes are distinct choices.

Wildlife. Kruger, Addo and Kgalagadi differ in landscape and safari experience. The coast adds penguins and seasonal whales; Sodwana brings coral reefs. Animal sightings are never guaranteed.

People and history. San rock art, ancient Mapungubwe, apartheid and contemporary city life reveal more than scenery alone. Wine valleys add architecture and food.

When to visit

Cape Town and the Western Cape: November–March is generally warmer and drier; June–August is cooler and wetter. Wind can stop cable-car and boat services.

Northeastern safaris: May–September is usually drier with thinner vegetation; summer brings greener scenery, rain and insects. The Drakensberg can have summer thunderstorms and winter frost or snow.

Seasonal experiences: Hermanus whales are most often seen from June to November; Namaqua flowers typically appear in late winter and spring after suitable rain. Check conditions in the actual year.

Travel essentials

The currency is the rand (ZAR). English is widely used in tourism, within a multilingual country. Drive on the left. Sockets are types M/N, with D also found. Cities and major tourism routes have developed services; remote areas can have limited communications, fuel and medical care.

Robbery is a significant risk: agree safe approaches with your accommodation, keep belongings out of vehicles and avoid night driving and solitary walks in unfamiliar areas. Leave safari vehicles only at authorised points and never feed baboons. Hippos and crocodiles are hazards around northeastern rivers. Sharks and strong currents occur along the coast: choose monitored beaches and follow flags and closures.

Check vaccination advice before departure and discuss malaria prevention if visiting affected parts of Limpopo, Mpumalanga or northeastern KwaZulu-Natal. Bring repellent when needed. Reserve popular parks and tours ahead; prepare offline maps, insurance and the documents required for your nationality. Safety and preparation, malaria areas.
